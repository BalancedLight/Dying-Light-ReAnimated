## Open Dynamics Engine (ODE) 0.16.6

The secondary-motion preview embeds a Win64 collision wrapper linked to the
ODE 0.16.6 static library. The pinned upstream source is available from
https://bitbucket.org/odedevs/ode/downloads/ode-0.16.6.tar.gz and has SHA-256
`c91a28c6ff2650284784a79c726a380d6afec87ecf7a35c32a6be0c5b74513e8`.

ODE is dual-licensed; this build uses the BSD-3-Clause option. The build enables
ODE's bundled ODER Utilities TLS support with its BSD option. Trimesh backends,
GIMPACT, libccd, demos, and DrawStuff are disabled in this integration.
